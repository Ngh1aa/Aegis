Getting here

By train
The valley line runs every hour from the city and takes 45 minutes. Our driver meets every train at the terminus; book the transfer as an extra or call reception.
By car
Follow the valley road to its end and park in the garage at the foot of the hill. A lit tunnel through the rock brings you up beneath the hall. Parking is free for guests.
By air
The nearest airport is an hour and three quarters away by train, with one change in the city.
