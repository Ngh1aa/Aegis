A day in thehigh valley.

Aegis is a century-old hotel at the end of the valley road: forty-two rooms, a hall of pink marble, a long bar and a wall of whisky.

Plan your stay Begin the day

15:40

The way in is through the mountain.

Leave the car at the foot of the hill. A lit passage runs straight into the rock and brings you up beneath the hall.

16:10

The hall

Pink marble columns, a long run of arches and the armchairs everyone ends up in.

19:00

The long bar, built for the hour the peaks turn pink.

A counter runs the length of the glass. Order something cold and watch the light leave the cliffs.

Open
4pm–1am daily
Seats
36 at the glass

22:00

A wall of single malts, poured by the dram.

After dinner the bartender moves to the back room, where the bottles go to the ceiling and the ladder is part of the service.

  1. 01

    Peated, from the islands

    Smoke and sea salt; the one guests come back for.

  2. 02

    Sherry cask, from Speyside

    Dried fruit and walnut, best with the chocolate plate.

  3. 03

    Rye, from down the valley

    Spice and pepper from a distillery two villages away.

Guided tastings at 9pm, Thursday to Saturday.

06:40

The valley wakes slowly.

Mist sits on the meadows until the sun clears the eastern ridge, usually just after seven.

08:00

Breakfast outside, with the whole valley.

From May to October the tables go out onto the gravel terrace. In winter the same view comes through the hall windows.
