Privacy, terms and credits

Privacy policy

We use the name and email you give us only to hold your room and send your confirmation. We never sell your details or share them outside the hotel.

Booking terms

Room prices include VAT and breakfast. A local tourist tax of €4 per adult per night is added at checkout.

A booking request is confirmed by email; nothing is charged until you arrive. Cancel free of charge up to 72 hours before arrival; later cancellations are charged one night.
