Privacy policy

We use the name, email and phone number you give us only to hold your room or table, send confirmations and, if you agree, our newsletter. We never sell your details or share them outside Aegis.

To see, correct or delete your details, call 0235 3 900 900.

Booking terms

Room prices include 8% VAT and a 5% service charge. A booking request is confirmed by email; nothing is charged until arrival.

Cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged one night.

Restaurant tables are held for 15 minutes after the booked time.
